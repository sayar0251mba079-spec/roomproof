Our verification checklist

What “verified” actually means.

A listing goes live only after a team member has walked through the PG and filled in every line of this checklist. Each listing shows the date it was last checked.

If a room doesn't match its listing when you visit, tell us. We'll fix or remove the listing, and that PG will not be shown again until it's re-checked.

  • Real, dated photosTaken by our team of the actual room, washroom and kitchen, never supplied by the owner.
  • The full monthly costRent, food, electricity, laundry and the deposit, written down with the owner.
  • Distance to campusWalking time and auto fare, measured from the PG gate to the DGU gate.
  • Safety basicsGate, CCTV, warden or caretaker, lighting on the approach road, entry timings.
  • Water, Wi-Fi and foodWater supply hours, a speed test on the Wi-Fi, and a look at the menu and kitchen.
  • House rulesGuests, curfew, notice period and deposit refund terms, in writing.

Questions

Good to know.

Is RoomProof really free for students?

Yes. Students and parents never pay us. The PG owner pays a placement fee only when a student books and moves in.

How is this different from a broker?

A broker is paid by commission and usually shows the places that pay them. We list every PG we've verified, show the full cost and tenant reviews, and take no fee from you.

Which areas do you cover?

We are starting with PGs and rooms within about 3 km of DGU, and will expand to other Dehradun college areas after the pilot.

Do I pay the deposit or rent through RoomProof?

No. You pay the owner directly after your visit, once you're happy. We never handle your money.

What if the room isn't what the listing showed?

Tell us on WhatsApp. We'll re-check it, correct or remove the listing, and help you find another room on your shortlist.

My parents are choosing for me. Can they use it?

Absolutely. Parents can shortlist rooms and request visits on your behalf, and we'll keep you both updated on WhatsApp.
